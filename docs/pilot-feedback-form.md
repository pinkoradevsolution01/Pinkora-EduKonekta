# Pilot feedback form

Use one form per participant after two weeks and at pilot close. Do not request safety-report, guidance-case, student, or family details.

1. Role and participating class/area (optional):
2. Which task did you complete most often?
3. Could you complete it on your phone browser? Yes / No / Not tested
4. Rate ease of sign-in, navigation, communications, attendance/assignments, and reporting from 1–5.
5. Did any screen create an accessibility barrier? Describe the screen and assistive need without naming a student.
6. Did you see information you should not have been able to access? Stop using the affected screen and contact support immediately.
7. What was confusing, slow, or unreliable?
8. What one change would help your role most?
9. May the pilot team contact you for clarification? Preferred school contact method:

The facilitator records feedback as a triage item using [bug-triage.md](bug-triage.md).
