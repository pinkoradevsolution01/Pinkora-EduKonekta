# Pilot training and attendance record

Training uses demonstration data only. Each participant must complete the relevant practical task and the trainer records date, trainer, participant, role, and result outside this repository.

## Administrator (60 minutes)

1. Sign in, sign out, recover an account, and identify the support contact.
2. Invite a test user; verify the role before sending the invitation.
3. Create a school year, class, subject, enrollment, teacher assignment, and approved parent-student link.
4. Publish a school/class announcement and review read/acknowledgement status.
5. Review dashboard aggregates, date-filtered reports, CSV export permissions, and audit activity.
6. Change a non-sensitive school setting/feature flag, explain its audit entry, and identify who may change subscriptions.
7. Explain account deactivation, incident reporting, and the rollback contact.

## Teacher (45 minutes)

1. Sign in and confirm only assigned classes are shown.
2. Publish a class announcement and create an assignment.
3. Record attendance, correct an entry with a reason, and verify no duplicate sheet is created.
4. Add respectful, structured progress feedback and distinguish parent-visible from internal information.
5. Send an appropriate parent message without including sensitive information in a notification preview.
6. Report a suspected access issue and identify the school support contact.

## Guidance personnel (45 minutes; only when in scope)

1. Review the school's safeguarding policy and emergency escalation process.
2. Confirm case access is limited to assigned/authorized personnel; do not share case data during training.
3. Create a fictional training case, assign it, add an internal note, and close it according to policy.
4. Demonstrate restricted export approval and audit review.

## Completion gate

The school administrator confirms that every participating administrator and teacher completed their role checklist. Guidance features remain off until the guidance checklist and authorization review are complete.
