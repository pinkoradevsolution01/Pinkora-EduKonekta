# Pilot user manual

## All users

Use the school-issued invitation, choose a strong password, and sign out of shared devices. Use **Recover** if access is lost. Report unexpected access, missing records, or suspicious messages to the school's named support contact; do not send confidential case details in email or notification replies.

## Administrators

Use School administration to set up users, academic structure, verified parent links, and teacher assignments. Publish announcements only after checking audience and expiry. Use the dashboard for tenant-scoped aggregates; reports and CSV exports are limited to authorized administrators. Audit logs are review-only and cannot be edited through the application.

## Teachers

Work only in assigned classes. Record one attendance sheet per class/date, use the correction flow for changes, and write factual, respectful feedback. Parents can view only approved information for linked children. Use messaging for school communication; blocked attachments and unsafe content are rejected.

## Parents and students

Parents can view only approved links to their children. Students see their own school work. Contact the school directly for an urgent safety emergency; the platform does not provide emergency response.

## Guidance staff

Use confidential reporting and guidance cases only when the school has authorized the workflow. Do not export or disclose case information outside policy. General dashboards and notifications intentionally exclude case details.
